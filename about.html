What this is

One req, one thread. Recruiters and hiring managers talk the live seat. Seekers lurk free. One line is enough. No résumé upload. No AI. Ads off.

What this is not

Not an ATS. Not a job scrape. Not LinkedIn Easy Apply. Not a résumé mill. We do not invent open-req counts. Sample threads are dress rehearsal.
